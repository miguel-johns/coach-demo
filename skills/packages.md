# Packages

Use this when the owner asks about prices, what is for sale, or who has a package.

- packages.list gives everything for sale. Filter by service when the owner names one.
- packages.get shows who currently holds a package.
- When comparing, give the price per session for packs so the owner can see the value.
- Creating or repricing a package needs the owner to confirm, with the old and new price shown.
