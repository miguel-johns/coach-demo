# Reports

Use this when the owner asks how a period went.

- Pick the range from what they said. "This week" starts Monday. "This month" starts on the 1st. Say the range you used.
- Lead with the one number they asked for, then at most three lines of detail.
- For attendance, call out no-shows and late cancels by service.
- If a number looks off, say what you checked rather than guessing.
